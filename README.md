# Qualiphy async conversion demo

Clickable walkthrough of a proposed feature: one exam runs as a video visit or an async review, decided per invite by three switches, and the patient can always choose video. All data is illustrative; nothing is sent anywhere.

Open the live page: https://sirbrentz.github.io/qualiphy-async-conversion-demo/

- Start the walkthrough (20 steps, about 5 minutes) or explore on your own.
- The bottom bar switches between the SuperAdmin, Clinic, Patient and Provider views.
- Changes stay in your browser. **Reset** starts over.

Generated from a private working repo; edits belong there, not here.
